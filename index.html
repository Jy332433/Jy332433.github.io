Junyu Ren

PhD student at the University of Chicago. I work at the AI–math interface in two directions: building AI-(co)-mathematician systems to tackle long-horizon, heavy-machinery open problems, and developing mathematical tools and novel frameworks rooted in pure mathematics to mitigate hallucination, characterize complexity limits, and advance mechanistic interpretability.

About

I'm a second-year PhD student in Computational and Applied Mathematics at the University of Chicago, advised by Lek-Heng Lim. I also work with Risi Kondor and Victor Veitch. My research sits at the intersection of applied mathematics, neural-network theory, mechanistic interpretability, and AI-augmented scientific work. Before UChicago, I read Mathematics and Philosophy at Oxford.

Workflow tools

ccrender — chat renderer with a VS Code extension companion for Claude Code. I built it for the moments where the CLI's rendering falls short: real-time LaTeX in mid-conversation clarifications, and markdown tables, ASCII flowcharts, or mermaid diagrams that don't display cleanly in the terminal. A small piece of tooling, but it noticeably changed my day-to-day Claude use.
